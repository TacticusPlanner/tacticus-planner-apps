# Known Issues

Things to expect while using Tacticus Planner during alpha testing. Most of these don't block normal use — they're noted here so they're not a surprise. The exception is the first one below, which can interrupt a page until you refresh it.

- **A page can occasionally get stuck showing "loading"** if your device has trouble reading its locally saved data. If a page seems to hang on loading longer than expected, try refreshing.
- **You might briefly appear signed in and then get asked to sign in again.** This can happen if your saved session has expired since your last visit.
- **Machines of War and NPCs library pages are minimal for now** — they show basic stats without the fuller detail views that Characters, Guild Raid Bosses, and Shops already have. More detail is coming.
- **Character, item, and upgrade names are shown in English for everyone**, regardless of your chosen language. Everything else in the app is translated.
- **A few abilities have no rules text yet**: the relics Fidelity, Lion Helm and Thrydderghyre, and the abilities Masterful Tactician, Power Vomit, Waaagh! Energy, Prime Test Subject and Diabolic Regeneration. Their panels show the name with a short note instead.
- **Master-Crafted Power Field and Artificer Power Field show a placeholder icon**, because the game data has no artwork for them.
