## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Raids Plan includes Upgrade goals

The Raids Plan and Schedule SHALL include the farming need of Active Upgrade goals in the same
global priority order and daily energy budget as every other goal, using the same demand as Today.

#### Scenario: Upgrade goal on later days

- **GIVEN** an Active Upgrade goal whose need does not fit in today's energy
- **WHEN** the Schedule is shown
- **THEN** its remaining raids appear on following days after higher-priority goals
