# Source notes

Severyn's horde-mode strategy notes (thread message, 2026-10-18), copied as the source for `hordeStrategy` (en). Proposed balancing text, not verified game mechanics: review before shipping.

## S

- **Kariyan** (custoBladeChampion): Position adjacent to multiple enemies to maximize Legacy of Combat. Avoid adjacency to Big Targets when you want the area attack rather than the single-target Piercing attack. His reusable active becomes more powerful over prolonged battles.
- **Isabella** (adeptHospitaller): Place behind a compact formation of non-Mechanical allies to keep them alive through repeated attacks. Use her active to resurrect fallen Imperial allies when necessary.
- **Nauseous Rotbone** (deathRotbone): Keep adjacent to frontline Chaos characters, particularly Maladus or Angrax. His passive can save Chaos allies from lethal damage, including overkills.
- **Aleph-Null** (necroSpyder): Anchor Mechanical teams and repair Re'Vas or other valuable Mechanical units. Use Scarabs to absorb attacks and occupy enemies.
- **Re'Vas** (tauCrisis): Position where multiple enemies must enter her Overwatch range. Deploy Shield Drones and pair with Aleph-Null. The DW-02 Advanced Burst Cannon relic allows repeated use of her active after enough attacks.
- **Maladus** (deathBlightlord): Position at narrow passages, where his Haze of Corruption can chain kills into adjacent enemies. Pair with Rotbone for longevity. Daemonflesh Plate greatly improves his late-game endurance.
- **Tyrant Guard** (tyranTyrantGuard): Keep beside fragile allies and avoid unnecessary movement so Guardian Organism provides damage reduction. Particularly effective in defensive formations.
- **Tan Gi'da** (admecMarshall): Maintain Protector Imperative while building a Skitarii army. Switch to Conqueror Imperative when additional attacks are more valuable. Pair with Actus and other Mechanical characters.
- **Snotflogga** (orksRuntherd): Attack consistently to produce Grots, then use his active when enemies surround him. Summons distract enemies and multiply. Supa-Cybork Body also makes him repairable.
- **Brother Jaeger** (templChampion): Place in melee chokepoints so attackers trigger Martial Superiority before their attacks resolve. Increase his passive ability level and protect him against ranged enemies.

## A

- **Boss Gulgortz** (orksWarboss): Use WAAAGH! to summon Ork Boyz, improve allied melee attacks, and charge into enemies. His follow-up attack provides consistent extra damage.
- **Angrax** (blackTerminator): Deep Strike near enemy spawn points. His passive attacks enemies moving from adjacent hexes, potentially clearing entire waves automatically. Prioritize passive upgrades and support with Rotbone, Wrask, or Abaddon. His passive triggers when an enemy moves from an adjacent hex, not simply when an enemy spawns; positioning him beside spawn points works because enemies often move out of those adjacent hexes. At higher difficulties, his damage can stop being sufficient without upgrades and Chaos buffs.
- **Sho'syl** (tauMarksman): Keep behind the frontline and continuously deploy Sniper Drones. Use Markerlights to focus dangerous enemies. Protect Sho'syl because losing him removes your ability to replenish drones.
- **Bellator** (ultraInceptorSgt): His Inceptors provide additional damage and bodies to distract enemies. Wait for later turns when practical to improve summon quantity, and avoid trapping your team with summons.
- **Marneus Calgar** (ultraCalgar): Position adjacent to damage dealers to benefit from Rites of Battle. Use his active against dense enemy formations. His Gravis armour provides additional survivability.
- **Actus** (admecManipulus): Repair Mechanical allies to support them and generate additional attacks. Particularly effective with Tan Gi'da, Re'Vas, and Mechanical summons.
- **Tyrith** (custoKyrus): Use her reusable active for positioning, then let her counterattack enemies. Pair with Isabella. Her passive requires enemies to attack her, so she is less effective when ignored or overwhelmed.
- **Nicodemus** (bloodSanguinary): Keep close to non-Mechanical allies for regeneration. With Chalice of Baal, nearby deaths trigger significant additional healing and melee damage buffs.
- **Brother Burchard** (templAggressor): Use ranged attacks against clustered enemies to spread Boltstorm damage and Suppression. Excellent defensive character with Gravis armour.
- **Wrask** (worldTerminator): Deep Strike alongside other Chaos frontline units. Kill enemies in melee to repeatedly generate shields for nearby Chaos allies.
- **Anuphet** (necroOverlord): Delay Resurrection Orb until it can generate multiple Necron Warriors. His Phasal Subjugator relic provides additional summon and attack opportunities.
- **Titus** (ultraTitus): Use as a durable frontline character whose passive can prevent defeat. Prioritize opportunities to secure kills and maintain survivability.
- **Vindicta** (adeptRetributor): Target dense groups with her Flame attacks and active. Excellent area damage, but avoid blocking allied movement with Burning hexes.
- **Typhus** (deathTyphus): Durable Psyker with effective area damage. Lakrimae substantially improves his Horde performance by generating Poxwalkers.
- **Abraxas** (thousInfernalMaster): Activate Malefic Maelstrom, then coordinate Psychic attacks that turn into additional Screamer summons. Keep available adjacent hexes for summons.
- **Cezare** (bloodTerminator): Engage enemy clusters to maximize his repeated melee damage and self-sustain. Effective frontline wave clearer, especially with healing support.

## B

- **Gibbascrapz** (orksBigMek): Support Mechanical allies through repairs and his defensive aura. Deploy the Grot Tank to absorb damage and provide ranged attacks.
- **Archimatos** (blackPossession): Produce Bloodletters to overwhelm enemies and divert incoming attacks. Works well in Chaos/Psyker teams but is vulnerable without protection.
- **Aesoth** (custoVexilusPraetor): Defensive support whose armour bonuses and damage mitigation help nearby allies. Best in formations that need extra survival rather than damage.
- **Incisus** (ultraApothecary): Provide healing and revival for non-Mechanical units. Particularly valuable when stronger faction healers are unavailable or restrictions require Piercing damage.
- **Imospekh** (necroDestroyer): Use Overwatch against predictable enemy movement and defend with Mechanical support. Can be effective against incoming waves but needs protection.
- **Lucius** (emperLucius): Useful frontline survivor with revival-related utility. Take advantage of his durability to occupy enemies and preserve more fragile allies.
- **Toth** (thousTerminator): Self-sustaining Chaos character that can survive prolonged battles. Works well in defensive positions with environmental effects and Chaos support.
- **Corrodius** (deathBlightbringer): Supports Chaos teams through summons and attrition. Orbs of Decay can considerably improve his effectiveness against clustered enemies.
- **Xybia** (genesMagus): Generates repeated summons that distract enemies and gradually overwhelm less durable waves. Keep her protected while summons do the work.
- **Mataneo** (bloodIntercessor): Use repeated charges to produce temporary summons that absorb enemy attacks. More effective as mobile disruption than a stationary tank.
- **Abaddon** (blackAbaddon): Place within buffing range of Chaos damage dealers. He improves characters such as Angrax and Wrask; Talon of Horus adds significant wave-clearing potential.
- **Eldryon** (eldarFarseer): Keep enemies inside the range of Doom so allied attacks deal additional damage. A strong amplifier, but lacks the sustain to carry waves alone.
- **Commander Farsight** (tauFarsight): Mobile ranged damage dealer. With Dawn Blade, securing kills also supports his survivability.
- **Makhotep** (necroWarden): Supports Necron and Mechanical formations with sustain and activation utility. Most effective when other Mechanical allies provide the damage.

## C

- **Thaddeus Noble** (astraOrdnance): Suppress dangerous ranged enemies and use artillery against groups. Strong initial control, but limited long-term sustain.
- **Haarken Worldclaimer** (blackHaarken): Mobile damage dealer who can accumulate power from kills. Needs protection, particularly against high-damage waves.
- **Varro Tigurius** (ultraTigurius): Useful early area Psychic damage and defensive utility. Less reliable against prolonged high-level enemy waves.
- **Sibyll Devine** (astraPrimarisPsy): Provides protection against ranged attacks and additional Psychic damage. Primarily a niche support selection.
- **Roswitha** (adeptCanoness): Stronger against particular enemy types and targets. Limited sustained wave clearing outside favorable matchups.

## D

- **Thutmose** (necroPlasmancer): Can provide damage in specific situations, but lacks the defensive tools and reliable wave control needed for extended fights.
- **Certus** (ultraEliminatorSgt): Long-range single-target damage and occasional control. Low attack frequency and limited endurance make him a poor general Horde investment.

D does not mean useless across the game, only limited in horde modes.

## Relic advice

- **Re'Vas**, DW-02 Advanced Burst Cannon: allows repeated use of her active after enough attacks.
- **Maladus**, Daemonflesh Plate: greatly improves late-game endurance.
- **Angrax**, Daemonflesh Plate: grants armour from attacks and eventually refreshes the active ability.
- **Snotflogga**, Supa-Cybork Body: makes him repairable.
- **Anuphet**, Phasal Subjugator: additional summon and attack opportunities.
- **Nicodemus**, Chalice of Baal: nearby deaths trigger additional healing and melee damage buffs.
- **Typhus**, Lakrimae: generates Poxwalkers.
- **Abaddon**, Talon of Horus: significant wave-clearing potential.

## General

- Survival summons do not increase their rarity when the battle's rarity cap rises; summons created early can be underpowered in later waves. Mention this for summoners.
- Onslaught and Survival have faction or alliance restrictions and LRE tracks have objective restrictions; the multiplier stays static and eligibility is evaluated separately.
