## Why

Review of the alliance battle suggestions found two gaps. The totals still said "raids" for battles fought by hand. With the featured hero unresolved, the alliance node filter was dropped, so nodes of any alliance could be listed.

## What Changes

- Battle suggestions label their totals, energy budget and no-drop note as battles.
- All-alliance suggestions wait for the featured hero's alliance and say so meanwhile.

## Impact

- `features/daily-raids/ui/hre-missions`, `pages/dailies/ui/hre-page.tsx`, `hre.json` locales and tests.
