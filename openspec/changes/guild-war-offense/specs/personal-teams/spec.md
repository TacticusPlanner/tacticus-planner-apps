## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The War Offense section links to Guild War Offense

On the Teams page the War Offense mode section SHALL show, in its header, a link that opens Guild War Offense (`/guild/war/offense`). Other mode sections SHALL show no such link.

#### Scenario: Open Offense from Teams

- **GIVEN** a War Offense team on the Teams page
- **WHEN** the user taps Open Guild War Offense in that section's header
- **THEN** `/guild/war/offense` opens
